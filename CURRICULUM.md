# Curriculum and decision mapping

Windshift Riders — רוכבי הרוח is an original arcade geography game for Hebrew-speaking grades 8–9. Workbook defines the requested content scope. The scenarios, terrain, units, weather fields and tuning are authored approximations; this is neither an exact map nor flight instruction. No Ministry approval or measured learning gain is claimed.

## Sources

The supplied snapshots are preserved in `docs/curriculum/`. Canonical source URLs:

- **W — Weather and local winds:** [תופעות מזג אוויר: רוחות ומשקעים](https://geography.workbook.co.il/grade-8/chapter-3/weather-phenomena), grade 8; snapshot `g8-weather.md`.
- **S — Solar heating and radiation:** [השמש כמקור אנרגיה ומאזן הקרינה](https://geography.workbook.co.il/grade-8/chapter-3/sun-energy), grade 8; snapshot `g8-sun.md`.
- **C — Israel climate:** [אקלים ישראל](https://geography.workbook.co.il/grade-9/chapter-2/israel-climate), grade 9; snapshot `g9-climate.md`.
- **B — Israel relief:** [רצועות האורך ותהליכי עיצוב הנוף](https://geography.workbook.co.il/grade-9/chapter-2/landform-belts), grade 9; snapshot `g9-belts.md`.
- **F — Applied soaring extension:** [FAA Glider Flying Handbook, chapter 9](https://www.faa.gov/regulations_policies/handbooks_manuals/aviation/glider_handbook/gfh_chapter_9.pdf), checked 31 October 2026. This supports the extension that a glider sinks through its surrounding air, thermal availability depends on conditions beyond sunshine, and slopes can redirect wind upward on the windward face and downward in the lee. It is not a Workbook module. No real aviation operating limits are taught.

## What counts as evidence

A finish records successful course flight and landing. The default landing assistant is explicit and logged separately; it does not assess manual landing skill. A control medal requires at least one centred physical passage and a flight-quality threshold. An environment medal records the level's course-phase evidence without optional hints or slow assist; it does not claim that the student can verbally explain the cause. Tutorial wind/control coaching is part of the first levels, so their medal is not evidence of instruction-free discovery. Assisted approach cannot add environmental-decision credit. Hint use, retries and aided flights remain separate from the first unaided attempt. A recorded turn toward lift plus the resulting vertical speed is stronger gameplay evidence than selecting an answer. Feedback should state the observed consequence, never infer a student's beliefs.

Every row below is a design-and-review contract. Runtime implementation and results are reported in `PLAYTEST.md`; the table alone is not completion evidence.

## Source simplifications deliberately excluded from assessment

1. **Albedo is only one factor.** The solar module's same-latitude desert/forest example cannot establish actual surface temperature or thermal strength from color. Moisture, evapotranspiration, heat capacity, solar exposure, time and atmospheric stability also matter. The game uses explicit authored surface properties and matching-color counterexamples.
2. **The local pressure model is bounded.** The weather snapshot uses “always” for high-to-low flow. The game uses simplified local breezes under stated calm conditions; it does not assess this as a universal rule for large-scale atmospheric wind.
3. **Weather versus climate.** One short coastal sunset or wind change is weather. Regional wet/dry art and level18's rain-shadow explanation represent a schematic long-term pattern; no run shows a measured climate trend.
4. **Land/sea and valley reversals are conditional.** Day and night scripts are illustrative conditions, not an assertion that every Israeli coast reverses at every night regardless of larger weather systems.
5. **Elevation relationship is general.** The snapshots simplify snow and rain examples. No guaranteed snow threshold or universal rainfall-by-height formula is assessed.
6. **No greenhouse analogy assessment.** The solar source compares the atmosphere directly with heat retention in a closed vehicle. That analogy is not used by these missions.
7. **Relief is schematic and static.** Sea remains west of Israel-inspired inland highlands; an eastern lee is to the east under west-origin rain-bearing air. Flight-scale changes do not form mountains or change the country's geomorphology.
8. **Original units and tuning.** Displayed speeds, altitudes, lift strengths, time compression and medal thresholds are game values. The game cannot support real-world flight or weather prediction.

## QA requirements

Rule tests must alter wind/sun/moisture while leaving the scoring logic alone and show physical route/lift changes. No hidden “correct answer” variable should award a geography medal for picking a label. Test energy loss without external rising air, dive-to-speed trade, bank/stall stability, pause stepping, late-stage reachability, failure/retry and persistence. Actual browser inputs must cover stages 1, 3, 8, 11, 12, 14 and 24. These checks and their limits belong in `PLAYTEST.md`.
